## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Provide full authentication UI pages (Sign In, Sign Up, Forgot Password, Reset Password, Email Confirm handler, Sign Out handler) built on Next.js 16 App Router, Base UI primitives (no `asChild`), TanStack Form with Zod validation, and Supabase SSR client.
- **Onboarding:** Implemented Option A (Email invites & Password Auth), backed by the `handle_new_user()` DB trigger which automatically associates invited users with pre-provisioned groups and roles upon registration.
