## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Add a Supabase custom access-token hook that injects `app_roles`, `app_groups` and `app_permissions` into every issued JWT, so the UI can gate menus/buttons on permissions. RLS never trusts these claims (m1-rbac.md rule 3).
- **Contract Acceptance Criteria:**
  > `custom_access_token_hook` enabled in config.toml; a seeded user's JWT contains `app_roles` (including roles inherited through groups), `app_groups` and `app_permissions`; hook and `claims_for_user` not executable by `authenticated`.
