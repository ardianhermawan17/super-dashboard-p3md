## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Subscribe to Supabase Realtime postgres_changes on `tasks` and `board_columns` for the active board, invalidating the TanStack React Query cache so movements and edits in one browser reflect across all connected clients in under 2 seconds.
- **Components:**
  - `src/features/kanban/hooks/use-board-realtime.ts`: Client hook creating a channel `board:${boardId}` listening for `*` events on `tasks` and `board_columns` matching `board_id=eq.${boardId}`, invalidating `kanbanKeys.board(boardId)` and safely removing the channel on component unmount.
  - `src/features/kanban/components/kanban-board.tsx`: Integrated `useBoardRealtime(board?.id)` hook into the board component.
