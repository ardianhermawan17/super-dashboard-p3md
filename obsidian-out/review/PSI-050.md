## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Implement PostgreSQL migration M5 for the Kanban board system, establishing core tables, RLS policies, auto-membership triggers, assignment notification triggers, Realtime publication, and pgTAP automated test suites.
- **Components:**
  - `public.boards`: Board definitions with creator tracking and ownership.
  - `public.board_members`: User membership mapping.
  - `public.board_groups`: Group membership mapping allowing entire teams/departments access.
  - `public.board_columns`: Column definitions with fractional position indexing (`collate "C"`) and done state.
  - `public.tasks`: Task cards with priority checks (`low`, `medium`, `high`), assignee reference, fractional positioning (`collate "C"`), and timestamps.
  - RLS functions & policies: `is_board_member(p_board)` and `is_board_owner(p_board)` enabling selective visibility and management.
  - Triggers: `boards_add_creator` (automatically adds board creator to `board_members`) and `tasks_notify_assignee` (emits assignment notification via `public.notify`).
  - Realtime: Added `public.tasks` and `public.board_columns` to `supabase_realtime` publication.
