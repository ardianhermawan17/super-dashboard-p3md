## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Deploy Migration M4 establishing events, polymorphic audience targets (user, role, or group), automated invite/update notification triggers, and calendar feed tokens.
- **Components:**
  - `public.events`: Calendar events with `source` ('app' vs 'google'), recurrence rules (`rrule`), and start/end bounds.
  - `public.event_audience`: Polymorphic audience rows with check constraint `num_nonnulls(user_id, role_id, group_id) = 1`.
  - Visibility RPCs: `can_see_event()` and `is_event_creator()`.
  - Notification Triggers: `event_audience_invites` (statement-level trigger batch-notifying audience on insert) and `events_notify_update` (notifying on time/location updates for app events).
  - ICS Feed: `calendar_feed_tokens` table and `events_for_user()` function for service-role unauthenticated feeds.
  - pgTAP suite: `supabase/tests/calendar.test.sql`.
