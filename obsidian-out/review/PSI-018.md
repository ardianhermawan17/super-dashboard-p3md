## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Provide full user management capability for administrators under `/dashboard/admin/users`, powered by the `admin_list_users()` database helper, the `admin-users` Deno Edge Function, and RLS-checked Server Actions for role/group memberships.
- **Contract Guardrails:** All permission checks use permission keys (`users.read`, `users.manage`) instead of role checks (C-17). RLS blocks unauthorized privilege escalation automatically.
