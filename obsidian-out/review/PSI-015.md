**Notes on design decisions found while writing the tests:**
1. Fixtures (roles/groups/role-grant) are installed as `postgres` (bypasses RLS); assertions switch to `authenticated`/`anon` with `set local request.jwt.claims`.
2. `effective_role_ids`/`users_with_permission` are revoked from `authenticated` by M1 — so inheritance/suspension data assertions run as `postgres`, and §8 separately proves the functions throw `42501` for `authenticated`.
3. `can_grant_group` returns `true` for a role with **no permissions** (nothing to escalate) — test §4 gives the group's role `mail.send` so member3's lack makes granting fail.
4. RLS `using`-clause deletes are *silent* (0 rows), not exceptions — §6 asserts row count stays 1 rather than `throws_ok`.
