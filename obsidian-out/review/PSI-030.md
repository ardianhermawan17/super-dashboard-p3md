## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Deploy Migration M3 establishing database schema, RLS policies, and recipient resolution functions for Role and Group Mail ("one-liner").
- **Components:**
  - `public.messages`: Messages targeting exactly one role (`to_role_id`) XOR one group (`to_group_id`) with `mail_status` enum ('draft', 'queued', 'sending', 'sent', 'failed').
  - `public.message_recipients`: Per-recipient delivery status and provider message ID tracking without raw email storage.
  - Security Definer Helpers: `is_message_sender()` and `is_message_recipient()` breaking policy circular references.
  - RPCs: `mail_recipients()` (service-role only, resolving active user emails including role inheritance through groups) and `mail_recipient_count()` (authenticated role safe).
  - pgTAP suite: `supabase/tests/role_mail.test.sql`.
