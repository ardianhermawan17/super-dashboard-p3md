## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Implement PostgreSQL migration M7 establishing the trigger-fed `public.activity_log` read model consumed by MCP agent tools, automated digests, and audit feeds.
- **Table:** `public.activity_log` with `id bigint generated always as identity`, `occurred_at`, `actor_id references auth.users`, `entity_type in ('task','message','event','candidate')`, `entity_id`, `verb`, `summary`, `board_id`, `role_id`, `group_id`, `meta`.
- **Privacy & Summaries:** Summaries are clean single sentences without PII (`Task "X" created`, `Task "X" moved to In Progress`, `Mail "Y" sent to marketing`, `Event "Z" on 01 Oct 10:00 WIB`).
- **Trigger-fed Event Stream:**
  - `tasks_activity`: logs task creation, deletion, moves (with destination column title), and title/description updates. Position-only reorders are suppressed to prevent noise.
  - `messages_activity`: logs when email status updates to `sent` or `failed`, resolving the role or group slug.
  - `events_activity`: logs app-created events. Ignores external Google Calendar imports (`source = 'google'`).
- **Row-Level Security:** Select policy enforces entity visibility (`actor_id = auth.uid()`, `is_board_member(board_id)`, role membership, group membership, or `can_see_event(entity_id)`). No insert policy is exposed to clients; writes occur exclusively through security-definer triggers.
- **Data Retention:** `pg_cron` schedule `activity-retention` runs daily at 20:30 to purge entries older than 180 days.
