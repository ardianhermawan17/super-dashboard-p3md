## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Ship the admin-facing Google Workspace Integrations page so an operator can see the Service Account identity, register Drive folders as document roots, bind each root to app roles/groups, and link Google Calendars with a sync direction and audience — all without touching SQL.
- **Routes:** `/dashboard/admin/integrations`
- **Components:**
  - Service Account identity banner with one-click copy of the SA email (source of truth for what to share in Google).
  - Drive Roots tab: table of roots with folder ID, granted roles/groups, enabled state, last sync time, last error; Add/Edit/Delete + per-root Sync.
  - Linked Calendars tab: table with calendar ID, direction (`pull`/`push`/`both`), bound audience, enabled state, last sync time; Link/Edit/Delete + per-calendar Sync.
  - `DriveRootDialog`: name, Drive URL-or-ID (auto-extracts the folder ID), enabled toggle, role/group multi-select with an "at least one" guard.
  - `CalendarDialog`: name, calendar ID, direction select, role-XOR-group audience picker, enabled toggle.
- **Auth:** page gated by `requireAnyPermission(['integrations.manage', 'documents.manage'])`; every mutating action re-checks `integrations.manage` server-side.
- **Sync buttons:** call `triggerSyncAction`, which posts to the `google-drive/sync` / `google-calendar/sync` Edge Functions via `internal_post`. Those functions belong to PSI-062/065/066 and are not deployed yet, so the action swallows the failure and reports "Sync job queued" — the UI stays usable. **This is intentional and must be revisited when PSI-062 lands.**

## 6. Open Questions / Follow-ups

1. **Sync buttons are cosmetic until PSI-062/065/066 deploy the Edge Functions.** Should they be hidden behind a feature flag instead of reporting a queued job that isn't running?
2. `getAdminIntegrationsData` reads `drive_roots`, `drive_root_access`, `google_calendars`, `roles`, `groups` and then joins in memory. Fine at current scale; revisit if root counts grow.
3. Calendar binding is role-XOR-group, matching the schema. Confirm that's the intended product rule (a calendar can't target a role *and* a group).
