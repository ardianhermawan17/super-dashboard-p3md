## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Guarantee the RBAC system can never lock itself out: no one can escalate a grant beyond their own permissions, the last `roles.manage` holder is never removed, suspended users resolve to no roles, and users can only edit their own name/avatar/timezone.
- **Contract Acceptance Criteria:**
  > `can_grant_role` / `can_grant_group` in insert policies; last-admin statement triggers; suspended users resolve to no roles; users can update only their own name, avatar and timezone.
