## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Implement board sharing management for individual users (`board_members`) and whole groups (`board_groups`), allowing board owners to invite or revoke access, with group members automatically inheriting access via `is_board_member()` RLS logic.
- **Components:**
  - `src/features/kanban/actions.ts`: Added `getBoardSharingAction`, `addBoardMemberAction`, `removeBoardMemberAction`, `addBoardGroupAction`, and `removeBoardGroupAction`.
  - `src/features/kanban/components/board-share-dialog.tsx`: Tabbed modal interface enabling board owners to manage individual member lists and shared department/team groups with real-time add and remove actions.
  - `src/features/kanban/components/kanban-view-page.tsx`: Integrated `BoardShareDialog` into the Kanban page header.
