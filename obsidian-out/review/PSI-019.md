## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Provide full management pages for Groups (`/dashboard/admin/groups`), Roles (`/dashboard/admin/roles`), and Permissions catalogue (`/dashboard/admin/permissions`).
- **Features:**
  - **Groups:** Create/edit groups, manage members (direct toggle + email list paste), and manage group granted roles.
  - **Roles:** Create/edit roles, interactive permission matrix grouped by module (unauthorized checkboxes disabled), and direct/inherited holder counts.
  - **Permissions:** Read-only catalogue displaying permission key, module, description, and granting roles.
  - Token refresh note on all admin views.
