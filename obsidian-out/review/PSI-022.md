## 1. Feature Summary & Goal

- **Objective:** Provide device Web Push enablement and user preference management for notifications.
- **Components:**
  - `src/features/notifications/lib/push.ts`: Push support detector, VAPID key converter, `enablePush()` registering subscription with `register_push_subscription()` RPC, and iOS standalone detection (`needsInstallFirst()`).
  - `usePushSubscription()`: Hook managing permission states, push subscription lifecycle, and notifications toggles.
  - `EnablePushButton`: User-tap explicit activation button with fallback alerts for unsupported browsers and iOS home-screen installation hints.
  - `NotificationPrefsForm`: Interactive category toggles (`mail.received`, `event.invited`, `event.updated`, `task.assigned`, `digest.ready`, `document.added`, `system`) and registered device manager with deletion actions.
